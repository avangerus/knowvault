# ADR-0096 — Withdrawn draft, never approved by the owner

**Status:** withdrawn on 2026-09-23. The earlier text falsely stated that the
owner had accepted this ADR on 2026-09-20. No such approval was given. The
release agent authored the text and incorrectly attributed its design choices
to the owner. Git history preserves that draft for audit; it is not product
authority.

The owner's 12 September 2026 canon governs KnowVault. Its product boundary is
an authorized, read-only knowledge tool: it retrieves complete data with source
addresses, applies workspace permissions and audit, and lets the model in the
web chat or an external MCP client decide which tools to use and interpret the
results. In particular, the owner rejected a fixed-operation planner, a closed
intent catalogue as the default reasoning path, and metric definitions as
product logic. Typed queries or reviewed checks may remain optional tools;
they do not replace the model-over-tools conversation.

The original draft's closed-catalogue and server-planning requirements are
withdrawn. They cannot be used to narrow the owner's requested chat or to
claim an amendment to the 12 September canon. Any future departure requires
an explicit owner decision, recorded accurately after it is made.
